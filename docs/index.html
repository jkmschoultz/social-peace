Social Peace

Short, calming videos for a break from the scroll.

Social Peace is a small personal tool that makes short vertical videos of calm nature footage and ambient sound, with a single line of text such as "Unclench your jaw. Drop your shoulders."

The operator reviews each video by hand, then the tool posts it on a schedule to the operator's own accounts on YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikTok, using each platform's official API.

It is not a public service. It only ever posts to accounts its operator owns and has signed in with, and it has no other users.
