Privacy Policy

Social Peace · Last updated 25 September 2026

Social Peace ("the app") is a personal tool that posts short videos to its operator's own social media accounts. This policy explains what data the app handles when you connect a TikTok, YouTube or Instagram account to it.

Who uses the app

Only the operator uses the app, with accounts the operator owns. It does not offer sign-up, and it collects no data about viewers of the posted videos or about anyone else.

What data the app receives

When you sign in with TikTok and approve access, the app receives:

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens, which let it post on your behalf;
  • basic profile information (user.info.basic), such as your display name, username and account identifier;
  • the posting settings TikTok reports for your account, such as allowed privacy levels and maximum video length, which the app checks before uploading.

The app requests the video.publish permission only so that it can upload videos the operator has made and approved. It does not read your messages, followers, other videos, analytics or any other account data.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload and publish videos to the connected account, and to confirm that each upload succeeded. It is not used for advertising, profiling or analytics.

Where the data is stored

Tokens and profile information are stored only on the operator's own computer, in a local file that only the operator can read. Nothing is stored on a server run by the app. A local log records each video that was posted and the resulting post link.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share your data with anyone. Data is sent only to the platform you are posting to (for example TikTok's official API), as needed to publish the video.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until access is revoked or they expire. You can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app, under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. Deleting the local token file also removes all stored TikTok data from the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly process data about anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
